Actuarial intelligence for families

Know exactly how much insurance your family needs.

ShieldIQ takes a household's income, loans, dependents and existing cover, and works out the life, health, critical-illness and accident cover it needs, what the gaps cost to close, and which plan types fit. Insurers are compared on official IRDAI claim and service records.

What it does

One profile, every protection question answered with numbers you can check.

Coverage gap analysis

Life cover need from income replacement, loans and education goals, minus savings, against what you already hold.

Plan options, priced

Four ways to buy each type of cover, estimated for your age, city and lifestyle, with the best fit highlighted.

Insurer ranking

Three years of IRDAI claim settlement, speed, complaints and solvency, weighted for your household.

Risk simulator

Stress-test early death, critical illness and disability, and see when the money runs out.

Family & health planning

Per-member cover for partners, children and parents, health conditions, and tax savings under both regimes.

AI explanations

Optional Gemini or OpenAI assistant that explains results, answers questions and reads policy PDFs.

English & हिंदी

Full Hindi localisation, light and dark themes, and WCAG 2 AA accessibility from 320px to 2560px.

Saved plans

Save strategies, compare variants, print a summary or export to CSV.

Run it locally

Needs Node 22, pnpm 10 (corepack enable). No database setup: an embedded Postgres is used when DATABASE_URL is unset.

git clone https://github.com/de-ansh/sheildIq.git && cd sheildIq
cd backend && pnpm install && pnpm dev    # API on :5001
cd ../frontend && pnpm install && pnpm dev # app on http://localhost:5173

Sign in with the one-click demo button, or alex.vance@shieldiq.internal / Password123!. Add GEMINI_API_KEY or OPENAI_API_KEY to backend/.env for AI features. Docker, deployment and configuration are covered in the README.

Figures are guidance, not regulated financial advice. Premiums are ShieldIQ estimates, not insurer quotes. Insurer records come from the IRDAI Handbook on Indian Insurance Statistics and Annual Report, FY 2024-25.
