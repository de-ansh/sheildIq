Methodology & Analytical Assumptions

Projections are based on the ShieldIQ v4.2 Actuarial Model. Calculations assume a 4.5% annual medical trend inflation rate and a 2% demographic age-up adjustment. Geographic rating factors are based on 2024 CMS Market Data for the selected region. Risk gaps are identified using a Monte Carlo simulation of 10,000 potential health outcome scenarios based on the provided family profile. This tool provides estimates only and does not constitute a legal insurance contract.
